## Cloud Computing Reflection

**1. Which cloud platform impressed you most?**
The cloud platform that impressed me most is **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**. I was impressed by its wide range of services for computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and application development. AWS also provides flexible and scalable solutions that can support both small startups and large businesses.

**2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?**
AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) all provide cloud services that allow users to access computing resources through the internet. They all offer services for virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, security, artificial intelligence, and scalability. The three platforms also use a pay-as-you-go model and allow businesses to increase or decrease resources depending on their needs.

**3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?**
Business requirements are very important when selecting a cloud platform because every organization has different needs and budgets. For example, a university that uses Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory may benefit more from Azure because of its Microsoft integration. A company focused on AI and machine learning may prefer GCP, while a startup may choose AWS because of its wide range of flexible services.

**4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?**
Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud platform is not simply about selecting the most popular provider. I learned to analyze the client's budget, technology environment, performance requirements, scalability, security, and future goals before making a recommendation. This helped me understand how cloud solutions can solve real-world business problems.

**5. How has your GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved?**
My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has improved by allowing me to organize and document my cloud computing activities, diagrams, solutions, and reflections. It gives me a record of what I have learned and shows my progress throughout the course. It also helps me practice presenting technical work in a clear and professional way.
